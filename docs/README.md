# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management processes, roles, and working guidance. Use the documents below as a shared reference throughout a project's lifecycle.

OctoAcme begins with initiation: clarify the problem, goals, success measures, stakeholders, timeline, and initial risks, then decide whether the work is ready for planning. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified dependencies and risks. During execution, teams track progress on a project board, deliver small increments, and review outcomes at regular syncs and demos.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers set outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams communicate through standups, weekly delivery and stakeholder updates, and shared project artifacts. Risks and blockers are reviewed regularly and escalated from the team through the Project Manager and Product Lead to the sponsor as needed.

Quality is supported by pull request reviews, automated tests and linting in CI, security scanning, and integration, end-to-end, or manual QA where appropriate. Releases require readiness checks, smoke tests, deployment verification, and a rollback or mitigation plan. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, or incidents turn lessons into improvement actions with owners and due dates.
