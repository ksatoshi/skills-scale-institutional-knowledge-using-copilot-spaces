# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers help ensure that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality expectations through risk-based test planning and validation.

### Responsibilities
- Define test approaches and identify coverage needs for features and critical workflows
- Validate acceptance criteria and document defects, test results, and quality risks
- Coordinate integration and end-to-end testing with relevant teams
- Advise on testability, accessibility, and quality practices throughout delivery

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Collaborate with Developers on testability, automation, defect triage, and fixes
- Coordinate test milestones and communicate quality risks to Project Managers
- Share release-readiness evidence with the delivery team; release decisions remain with the designated decision-makers

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX / Product Designers translate user needs and product goals into clear, usable experiences and validate design assumptions.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and identify usability problems
- Create and maintain user flows, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Validate designs with users or other appropriate feedback methods
- Document design decisions, accessibility considerations, and unresolved questions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to connect user needs, product outcomes, and scope
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility and resolve implementation questions
- Collaborate with QA / Test Engineers to make usability and accessibility expectations testable
- Coordinate design reviews and dependencies with Project Managers

---

## DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers support reliable delivery and operation of services through infrastructure, deployment automation, and operational practices.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and environment reliability
- Define and monitor service-level indicators, alerts, and observability needs
- Advise on capacity, resilience, backup, and recovery requirements
- Support deployment readiness and help investigate production incidents

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on delivery pipelines, service instrumentation, and operational readiness
- Advise Product Managers on reliability trade-offs that affect user outcomes
- Work with Project Managers to plan release dependencies, communicate operational risks, and coordinate incident updates
- Coordinate with QA / Test Engineers on environments and production-like validation

---

## Security / Privacy Specialists

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Specialists help teams identify and address security, privacy, and compliance risks throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Identify relevant threat, data-protection, and compliance requirements
- Advise on security and privacy controls and review high-risk design or implementation choices
- Coordinate or recommend appropriate security testing and track findings to resolution
- Communicate residual risks and escalation needs to accountable decision-makers

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers on secure design, implementation, and remediation
- Partner with Product Managers to account for privacy and security requirements in scope and acceptance criteria
- Advise Project Managers on risk-register entries, review dependencies, and escalation timing
- Collaborate with QA / Test Engineers on security and privacy validation

---

## Data / Analytics Specialists

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Specialists help teams define instrumentation and evaluate whether delivered work achieves its intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into measurable events, data definitions, and analysis plans
- Advise on instrumentation, data quality, privacy-aware collection, and reporting
- Analyze results and explain limitations or uncertainty in the evidence
- Maintain or document dashboards and metric definitions as appropriate

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to define success measures and interpret product outcomes
- Work with Developers to implement and validate instrumentation
- Coordinate measurement milestones and communicate progress or data risks to Project Managers
- Collaborate with QA / Test Engineers to verify that tracking behaves as intended

---

## Role Assignment and Accountability
- Assign a clear accountable owner for each decision and deliverable; specialist roles provide expertise but do not automatically replace Product Manager or Project Manager accountability.
- On smaller teams, one person may cover multiple roles. Make the responsibilities and decision ownership explicit so important work is not left unowned.
- Involve specialists early when their expertise affects scope, acceptance criteria, delivery dependencies, or release readiness.
